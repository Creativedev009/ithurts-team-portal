Your day starts in the studio

Everything ItHurts posts is prepared, reviewed and scheduled in the studio. This page is the front door: open the studio, keep the handbook handy, and tick off the daily routine.

Open the studio Read the handbook

Studio address:

Every day

  • 2 reels (one comedy skit, one product skit)
  • 4 image posts (2 carousels, 2 photos)
  • 1 article (Ouch File or Pain Insight)
  • Long YouTube video on Tuesday and Friday

No more, no less. Each post has a different job: reach, product, sign-ups, traffic, community, shop.

The routine

  1. Morning (US Eastern): open Today, prepare it if empty, review each item.
  2. Approve what is good; reject or discard the rest.
  3. Afternoon: prepare Tomorrow.
  4. Check Skits and Image posts once a day.

Rules that never change

  • Every promotion is a story: character, twist, product as their move, payoff.
  • No medical claims. Never cure, treat, heal, prevent, diagnose or guarantee.
  • 3–5 hashtags. Reels 25 seconds or under. Hook headline in the first second.
  • Colour logo with the white glow, top-left, the whole video.
  • Scheduled posts are changed in Metricool, not in the studio.

Ask the owner for

  • A new password (there are no reset emails).
  • A higher daily allowance, or a Runway top-up.
  • Anything in Products, the shop, Settings or API keys.
