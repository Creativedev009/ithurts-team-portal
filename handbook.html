Your job

The studio writes and makes; you decide what goes out and keep the rhythm. You are the human review on every post.

Every day

  1. Run the daily mix: 2 reels, 4 image posts (2 carousels, 2 photos), 1 article, and the long YouTube video on Tuesday and Friday. Not less, not more.
  2. Prepare the day in the morning (US Eastern) and tomorrow in the afternoon, so nothing is rushed.
  3. Review every piece before it posts: watch the video, read each platform's caption, check the carousel. No medical claims, the brand voice, the product shown, the logo in place, 3–5 hashtags, reels under 25 seconds.
  4. Approve, reject or fix. Approve what is good, fix captions (free), send weak ones back with a note ("funnier hook", "product in the first shot"), reject the rest. Nothing posts without your click.
  5. Keep the skits moving: write scripts (free), check storyboards shot by shot, redraw a bad shot before animating, approve the finished reels.
  6. Make hand-made image posts when the day needs them: a sign-up push, a shop pick, a community question.
  7. Spend credits wisely: stay inside your daily allowance, never remake something that already spent credits without a reason, and tell the owner when Runway is low.

Every week

  • Check the Numbers tab: which characters and formats get views, saves and shares, and lean next week's skits toward them.
  • Keep the skit mix on target (30% talking animals, 20% relatable humour, 20% product integrations, 15% community, 10% education, 5% behind the scenes).
  • Keep the idea bank full: suggest ideas, fill the 7-day calendar.
  • Give each post a different job across the week: reach, product, sign-ups, website traffic, community, shop.

Always

  • Edit scheduled posts in Metricool, never let a day expire unposted, and send the owner anything that needs them (settings, shop, passwords, credits).
  • Flag anything that looks off: a product that no longer exists, a caption that sounds like a claim, a character behaving out of style.
  • If you also have the Instagram, TikTok and Facebook logins: reply to comments and messages within the day, and bring good questions back as skit ideas.

Not your job

Products and the shop, WordPress publishing, API keys, the daily mix settings and Runway top-ups stay with the owner. Writing from scratch stays with the studio: you direct and edit.

1. Logging in

The studio is a website. Open the address the owner gives you (for example studio.ithurts.com) in Chrome and log in with your email and password. Nothing is installed on your laptop.

  • Your login stays active for 14 days on that browser.
  • Your name and how many Runway credits you have left today are shown at the top right. Log out is next to it.
  • Forgot your password? There are no reset emails. Ask the owner, who sets a new one in Settings → Team.
  • After 8 wrong passwords the login pauses for 15 minutes.

The menu shows Today, Tomorrow, Assets and Brand. Two more pages open from links on Today: Skits (the "Cast & ideas" link) and Image posts (the "Image posts →" banner). Products and Settings belong to the owner; if you open one you are sent back to Today.

The routine

  1. Morning (US Eastern time): open Today. If nothing is prepared, click Prepare today's content. Preparing costs about 256 credits and starts straight away, with no second question, so click it once.
  2. Review each item as it becomes ready. Watch the video, read the captions for every platform, open the carousel preview. Fix captions where needed (free).
  3. Approve what is good. Approve & schedule sends it to Metricool at its planned time. Your click counts as the human review.
  4. Reject or discard what is not. Nothing is posted unless you approve it. A day that passes without approval is never posted.
  5. Afternoon: open Tomorrow and prepare it the same way, so there is time to review before the morning.
  6. Check Skits once a day for storyboards waiting for you and ideas to write, and Image posts for anything you made by hand.

Short on something? Today tells you ("This day is short of your daily mix"). Click Top up the day, read the cost, then Confirm — make them.

Buttons on every card

  • Approve & schedule opens a dialog that says where and when it will post. Live means a real post.
  • Reject never posts it.
  • Retry for a stopped item. Free retries run at once; paid ones show the cost first.
  • Start over makes it again from scratch (new captions, plan and media; the old one stays in Campaigns). The dialog has a box for what to change: "funnier hook", "show the product in the first shot", "warmer photo", "calmer tone". The writer follows your note.
  • Discard removes it from the day.
  • Details opens the full workspace for that item.
  • Caption tabs: one caption per platform. Instagram gets "link in bio"; Facebook gets the tracked link.

The caption check

Before a video is made, the captions are checked for claim wording and tone. If the box says "Caption QA flagged N lines":

  • HIGH lines must be edited: Edit captions → Save & check again. Editing is free.
  • MEDIUM lines can be accepted with Accept & continue if you are sure the wording is fine.
  • Check again is free, but if the captions now pass, the video is made and its cost is charged.

Day-level buttons

  • Make N new posts adds posts; the dialog shows the cost.
  • Start day over re-plans the whole day and charges again. Only shown while nothing is approved.
  • Discard day / Discard the rest drops what is left; drafts stay in Campaigns.
  • Daily mix settings opens the mix panel. You can read it and refresh Metricool's best times, but saving is the owner's; the server refuses it from your login.

4. Skits and reels

Skits are the comedy reels with the recurring 3D cast: Mitzi the Savage Cat, Gus, Brutus, Nana Pearl, Kevin, Pip, Rocco, and Maya, the ItHurts host. Writing is free; you see the cost before any video is made; you approve before anything is posted.

From idea to post

  1. Write. On Skits → Write the skit (free): pick the star, the format, an optional product and an idea. Or use Suggest 5 new ideas (free) on the Ideas tab, or the Calendar tab's Write all N open slots (free). The script appears as Script ready. Not happy? Rewrite… with a direction such as "funnier last line" (free).
  2. Draw the storyboard. Draw storyboard (~N credits) makes one still per shot, about 8 credits a shot (about 40 for a reel). Check that every shot reads: right character, right product, nothing odd. The card asks "Storyboard — does every shot read?"
  3. Animate. Animate (~N credits) makes the video: 15 credits per second, so about 420 for a 28-second reel. Wait for Ready for approval.
  4. Approve. Approve & schedule posts it to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube Shorts at its slot (Metricool's best hour, or 7 pm). A long YouTube video goes to YouTube only, and its 3 Shorts post one a day on the following days.

A shot that looks wrong after the storyboard is cheaper to fix than a whole reel. Under each still there is Redraw shot N: say what is wrong ("the product is missing", "Brutus looks angry, not sleepy") and confirm. One shot is about 8 credits. Animate only when every shot reads.

Formats

  • Tips episode (Maya, 3 clear points, about 24 s)
  • Maya Tries It, the long YouTube video (about 3 min + 3 Shorts)
  • Problem → chaos → product · Unsolicited advice · Expectation vs reality · Fake luxury ad · Product courtroom · Relatable chaos (no product)

5. Image posts

Photos and carousels for Instagram and Facebook, in the ItHurts look. Writing and previewing are free. A new photo from Runway costs about 8 credits and asks you first. Nothing is posted until you click Schedule.

Making one

  1. New post: type a short brief, pick the Job (Sign-ups, Website traffic, Community, Shop, Product sale, Other), the number of slides (1 is a photo, up to 6 for a carousel) and where it sends people (Free Pain Check-Up, Join free, Support groups, Invite & Earn, The Ouch Files, Pain Insights, Become a contributor, The shop, or another page). Then Write it for me (free) or Start blank.
  2. Edit each slide. Layouts: "Photo + text", "One product", or "Shop picks (up to 3)". Put *stars* around the punchline in the headline to highlight it. For a photo slide, write a photo prompt and click Make photo (~8 credits), or Upload my photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, under 12 MB). Never put the headline over a face.
  3. Preview. Make preview renders the slides at 1080×1350. If it says "You changed something since this preview", make it again.
  4. Caption: 2 to 4 short lines in the ItHurts voice, with one question to start comments. Instagram adds "👉 link in bio"; Facebook gets the tracked link.
  5. Schedule (US Eastern). Pick date and time, at least 10 minutes ahead, then Schedule to Instagram + Facebook. Best US times from Metricool: community 09:00 · sign-ups 10:00 · shop 11:00 · traffic 17:00 (Sunday community 19:00).

After scheduling, the post is locked. To change it, edit it in Metricool, or Duplicate it here and schedule the copy. Save keeps a draft; Delete removes a draft and its photos.

Image posts that the studio prepares on Today use the product's own shop photo at no cost; only a photo without a product costs about 8 credits.

6. The daily article

Each day has one article, alternating an Ouch File (a vivid first-person pain story) and a Pain Insight (what a condition feels like). The first reel of the day retells it. On the card, Read the full article shows the text.

  • Approve & schedule creates a WordPress draft. The studio never publishes articles itself; the owner publishes it from WordPress. The card then shows the draft link.
  • Retry and Start over on an article are free.
  • If the card asks "Which website category should this go in?", leave it: that choice needs the owner's login.

7. Assets and Brand

Assets lists the latest 200 finished videos, images and voice files from the daily content, with a Download link on each. Filters: All, Ready to post, Finished, Raw generations, Voice & sound. Skit videos and image-post slides are not listed there: skit videos play on the skit card, and slides open from the Image posts preview.

Brand holds the brand profile the writer follows: tagline ("Because no one should face pain alone."), tone (relatable, empathetic, playful, validating, informative), colours, the Montserrat font, audience and "never in our marketing". It also runs 7-day campaign ideas. Your login can edit these, but don't change the brand profile or start or stop a campaign without the owner's OK: every post the studio writes follows it.

8. Credits

Videos and photos are made with Runway, which is paid in credits. You have a daily allowance (set by the owner, 1,000 by default); it is shown at the top right and resets each day on US Eastern time. 9. Content rules

  • Every promotion is a story: a character, a twist, the product as their move, a payoff. No bare product ads.
  • Reels: a hook headline in the first second, highlighted captions, a pop card, the product visible, a dark product end card. Keep reels at 25 seconds or under; open with a POV line.
  • Hashtags: 3 to 5, no more.
  • No medical claims. Never cure, treat, heal, prevent, diagnose or guarantee. Say "many people use it for…". Health posts carry a "not medical advice" line; the article does too.
  • Affiliate links stay in the caption with #ad; the studio adds them. Don't remove them.
  • The logo is the colour version with the white glow, fixed top-left for the whole video. Never the white version or a white box.
  • Brand colours: #883621, #792314, #490C0B. Font: Montserrat.
  • YouTube is story or documentary style, never a sales pitch.
  • Ideas come from the Viral Video Factory cast and concept bank on the Skits page, not from generic scripts.
  • Scheduled posts are changed in Metricool, not in the studio.

10. Things that need the owner

The server checks every request, so these fail from your login even where a button shows:

  • Saving Daily mix settings (posts per day, times, platforms, credit cap).
  • Re-sync shop products.
  • Choosing a website category for an article.
  • Products, the shop, WordPress pages and publishing articles.
  • Settings: API keys, Team logins, passwords, your daily limit.
  • Raising the studio's daily credit cap and topping up Runway.

When you hit one of these, note the item and the day, and send it to the owner.
